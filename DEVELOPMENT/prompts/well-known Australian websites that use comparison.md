1. Finder — probably your #1 benchmark

Finder Australia – Share Trading

Finder compares 30+ Australian share-trading platforms, including fees, asset classes and platform features. It also promotes exclusive offers and "Go to site" conversion buttons.

More importantly, Finder explicitly states that it can receive a referral fee, commission or other payment when users click through, apply for, or take out products.

Its crypto section follows the same model:

Finder Australia – Cryptocurrency Comparison

This makes Finder extremely relevant to the platform you're considering.

2. Canstar — another major benchmark

Canstar – Online Share Trading Comparison

Canstar compares Australian trading platforms and clearly labels Online Partners. It states that some brands may pay Canstar a fee for referrals and provides links that send users to the provider.

Canstar also has a sophisticated ratings/awards system. Its 2026 share-trading assessment covered 27 providers and 56 products.

For your idea, I'd study Canstar's comparison UX + Finder's affiliate/conversion UX.

3. CoinSpot — interesting crypto affiliate model

CoinSpot Affiliate Program

This one is particularly interesting if crypto affiliate revenue is part of your plan.

CoinSpot says affiliates can receive commissions for three years after referred members verify, with its published structure including 1% on AUD instant Bitcoin/crypto buy/sell transactions and 0.5% on crypto-to-crypto trades.

That's a very different model from simply getting paid for a one-time lead.
